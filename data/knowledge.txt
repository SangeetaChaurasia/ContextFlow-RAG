[Password Reset]
Category: account

To reset your password, go to the login page and click the
"Forgot password" link.

Enter the email address associated with your account.
You will receive an email containing a password reset link.

The password reset link expires after 30 minutes.

If you do not receive the email, check your spam or junk folder.
If the email is still missing, contact customer support.


[Refund Policy]
Category: billing

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

To request a refund, open the Orders section of your account,
select the order you want to refund, and click the "Request Refund"
button.

Refund requests are reviewed within 2 business days.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.


[Order Tracking]
Category: shipping

You can track your order from the Orders section of your account.

Open the order you want to track and click "Track Order".

Once the order has been shipped, you will see the tracking number
and the latest shipping status.

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to appear after shipment.


[Change Email Address]
Category: account

You can change your email address from your account settings.

Open Settings, select Account Information, and update your email
address.

You may be asked to verify the new email address.

After verification, future account notifications will be sent to
the new email address.
